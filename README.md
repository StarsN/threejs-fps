Sable Yard is a round-based tactical FPS in three.js, Counter-Strike-style and fully original: two teams of five fight short rounds in a desert-industrial yard. Plant the charge at Site A or Site B and defend it until it blows, or eliminate the other team. Money earned each round buys rifles, SMGs, a shotgun, a sniper and grenades between rounds; gunfire rewards stopping to aim — recoil and spread punish firing on the move, and headshots pay. Single-player against bots that follow routes, hold lanes, and react to what they see and hear.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play. WASD moves, mouse aims, click fires, R reloads, Ctrl crouches, B opens the buy menu between rounds, E plants/defuses the charge, G throws a grenade, 1–6 switches weapons.
